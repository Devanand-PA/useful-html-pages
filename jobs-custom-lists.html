Works best for Greenhouse (boards.greenhouse.io/…) and Lever (jobs.lever.co/…) URLs — fetched directly, no proxy. Other sites go through a CORS proxy and may fail; the URL is still saved so you can fill in details manually.

Live search asks RemoteOK, Company Boards, Arbetsförmedlingen and Hacker News "Who is Hiring" at once and merges the answers, so one dead API cannot empty your results.
Company Boards pulls live postings from Greenhouse and Lever boards at Stripe, Airbnb, Figma, Vercel, Cloudflare, Datadog and more — each company's own careers feed, no API key needed.
🔗 URL mode fetches any job page through a CORS proxy and pulls the title, company, description and logo out of its OpenGraph / JSON-LD meta tags.
🖼 Screenshot mode turns a pasted / dropped / uploaded image of a posting into a card — fill in the title and you're done.
✎ Manual mode for jobs that don't have a URL or screenshot.
Drag results into any tier or saved list. Drop on a card (not just the row) to place it left/right of that card — the line shows where it lands.
Drag a card onto any tab above to copy it into that list.
Hover a card = full description & meta, with your notes right below it (dbl-click card to jump into notes).
Set a card's status (Applied / Interview / Offer / Rejected) in the tooltip — it shows as a colored badge on the card.
Right panel builds a career profile and suggests similar jobs from your top keywords and companies.
📝 General Notes at the bottom of the main area holds free-form notes shared across every list — strategy, contacts, deadlines, follow-ups. Collapsible, auto-saved, and included in the Markdown export.
